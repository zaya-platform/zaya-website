# Home marketing surface

- Target: `/` — `src/pages/index.astro`, with `src/components/Promo.astro`.
- Mode: **Persuade**.
- Visual authority: the existing ZAYA website and the user’s refinement brief.
- Workflow: **code-first** refinement. Preserve Astro; use an isolated, optional Three.js module for the user-requested 3D experience.
- Product facts: `../../PRODUCT.md`. Canonical visual decisions: `../../DESIGN.md`.

## Visitor job

Understand what ZAYA is being built to do, choose the shopper or merchant experience, review credible product previews and contact the team with a relevant enquiry. A successful email action opens the visitor’s email app; the website does not register them automatically.

## Page strategy

Header Explore ZAYA dropdown with four audience icon links → hero with an independent interactive scene and next steps → shopper/merchant benefits, authentic Addis photography and real app previews → shop-managed delivery → optional ZAYA Promo and brand meaning → clearly planned RIDE/Diaspora experiences → FAQ → guided enquiry/contact/footer.

Keep the actual product easier to find than the promo. Audience navigation must activate the appropriate tab, including direct hashes. Preserve useful existing anchors: `#experience`, `#ride`, `#diaspora`, `#promo`, `#film`, `#meaning`, `#faq` and `#contact`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Clear local-commerce product understanding followed by an honest enquiry action.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve ZAYA’s official mark, light surfaces, navy text, teal structure, orange actions, Poppins and authentic Addis Ababa portrait.

STORY: Show shopper/merchant outcomes, real demonstration screens, separate shop delivery, then future concepts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the compact Explore ZAYA control directly visible beside the brand, including on phones; it opens a single dropdown containing Customers, Merchants, Riders and Diaspora icon links. Desktop places the introduction and secondary copy beside the network. Phones follow a compact introduction → independent network scene → secondary copy and actions. The menu uses four columns on desktop and two on mobile/tablet, with a description and availability status for each audience. Opening it reserves header height and pushes content down instead of covering the model. Selecting an audience closes the panel, updates the scene highlight and navigates to the relevant details; the model remains mounted. “Explore the app” and “Talk to the ZAYA team” lead to content on this page; the real portrait remains in the product-introduction section. Verify header fit and scene readability in actual narrow and short viewport checks rather than assuming the layout guarantees it at every zoom setting.

FORM: Existing marketing homepage, refined in code; no new visual-world seed or image-composition exercise.

FINISH: Local implementation, bounded desktop/mobile review, recorded checks and aligned documentation, followed by publication to the existing zayaethiopia.netlify.app domain under the user's subsequent authorization. No repository push is part of this task.

## Interaction and media

Keep the header's native Explore ZAYA disclosure in normal Tab order with visible focus, Escape/focus return and outside dismissal. The expanded panel remains scrollable when constrained, reserves its height above the page and closes before destination scrolling. Audience links highlight the associated scene node and connection without replacing the scene. Essential links remain usable without 3D or JavaScript, with a normal-flow dropdown fallback. Preserve keyboard-operable tabs in the separate product walkthrough, screenshot enlargement with reliable close/focus return, FAQ disclosures, mobile navigation and reduced-motion support. Keep the delivery illustration distinct from RIDE, and Diaspora explicitly planned. The optional promo remains user-initiated with no initial video download, working quality selection and YouTube links.
